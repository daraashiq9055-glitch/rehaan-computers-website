A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Do I need computer experience?+

Our beginner courses start with the fundamentals. Explore Computer Essentials or use the course finder to choose a starting point.

Is ₹100 the complete course fee?+

No. ₹100 is the admission fee. Tuition is separate. Confirm the full fee and payment schedule for your chosen course with the institute.

Can I choose my batch?+

You can share a Morning, Afternoon or Evening preference. The centre will confirm timings and availability.

Can I make a payment in this preview?+

No. This is a design preview. It does not connect to admissions, payment services or the admin dashboard.
