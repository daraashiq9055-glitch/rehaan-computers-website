FAQ

Common Questions

Is my admission complete after I submit the form?

No. The form registers your application. You must visit the centre with 2 passport-size photographs and a photocopy of your Aadhaar card to complete your admission.

Is there a last date for admission?

No. Online admission stays open all year — there is no last date. A one-time ₹100 admission fee blocks your seat, allotted in the order applications arrive.

When do classes start?

As soon as your admission is confirmed — pay the ₹100 admission fee, complete document verification at the centre, and join the next Morning, Afternoon or Evening batch.

Why is there a ₹100 admission fee?

The ₹100 one-time admission fee reserves your seat and covers registration. Course fees are paid separately at the centre — the admission fee is not an advance or EMI.

What documents do I need to bring?

Two passport-size photographs and one photocopy of your Aadhaar card. Carry them when you visit the centre after applying online.

What is the course fee?

The fee is ₹999/- for the courses listed on this website. Payment is made at the centre when you complete your admission.

What is the attendance account for?

It signs you in to the Rehaan Computers attendance system so you can track your daily class attendance. You can create it in the application form or ask at the centre.

Which batches are available?

You can choose a Morning, Afternoon or Evening batch in the application form. Batches begin right after your admission is confirmed.
