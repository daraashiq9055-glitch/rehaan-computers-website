RC Learn

Your tuition fee balance on Android.

Get the student app

RC Learn is available through the centre during the pilot. Ask your admin for the APK, your username and your initial password.

  1. Open the APK supplied by the centre and tap Install.
  2. Open RC Learn and choose Student sign-in.
  3. Enter your centre-issued login, then open Fees. Check your admission number and pending monthly tuition.

Need help signing in?

Contact the centre with your admission number so an admin can check your account. Keep your password private.
